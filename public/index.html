This optimizes annualized trade return, not total profit or present value. Realizing the annualized rate requires comparable reinvestment opportunities each cycle. It is not a promised yield.

Model (from the verified contracts): claim fee c(t) = maxFee − (maxFee − minFee) · tHatk, tHat = (t − minDuration) / (maxDuration − minDuration), from UnlockReceipt.feeCurve(); the apyUSD vault also takes unlockingFee u upfront, so total cost(t) = 1 − (1 − c(t)) / (1 + u). Trade return r(t) = (1 − cost(t)) / (1 − discount) − 1; APY = (1 + r)365/t − 1. apyUSD does not accrue yield while unlocking. Gas, slippage and price impact are not included.
